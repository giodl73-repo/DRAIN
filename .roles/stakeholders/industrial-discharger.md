# Industrial Discharger

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the industrial customer whose wastewater load affects treatment capacity and permit compliance.

## Primary concerns

- Pretreatment limits, monitoring, and predictable discharge capacity (DIM-03/07).
- Whether industrial load consumes municipal capacity or drives upgrades (DIM-01/12).
- Compliance risk from pollutants, surcharges, and permit changes (DIM-03/12).

## Used during

- Capacity, pollutant-removal, pretreatment, and benefit-cost scoring; tier/SLA for industrial load assumptions.
