# Public-Health Officer

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the public-health agency concerned with sanitation exposure.

## Primary concerns

- Disease prevention from safe collection and treatment (DIM-02/03).
- Basement backups, overflows, and exposure during wet weather (DIM-05/06).
- Protection for vulnerable households and facilities (DIM-11).

## Used during

- Service coverage, overflow, public-health benefit, and tier/SLA review for all scales.
