# Downstream / Receiving-Water Community

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the people and places downstream of a discharge or overflow.

## Primary concerns

- Clean rivers, lakes, estuaries, coasts, and groundwater (DIM-10).
- Low CSO/SSO exposure, pathogen risk, and closure days (DIM-06/07).
- Whether upstream benefits impose downstream ecological or recreational burden (DIM-10/11).

## Used during

- Receiving-water, nutrient/pollutant, overflow, and design-proposal siting review with the receiving-water advocate.
