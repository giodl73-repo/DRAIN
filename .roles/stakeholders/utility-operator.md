# Utility Operator

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the operator who must run the plant, collection system, and compliance program every day.

## Primary concerns

- Operability under wet-weather, power, solids, and staffing stress (DIM-05/06).
- Asset condition, maintainability, and realistic redundancy (DIM-09).
- Permit compliance and reporting workload (DIM-03/12).

## Used during

- Operations, resilience, asset-condition, and tier/SLA feasibility review with the operations officer and regulatory realist.
