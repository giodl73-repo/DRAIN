# Ratepayer / Household

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the household paying for and depending on sewer service.

## Primary concerns

- Affordable, predictable rates and connection costs (DIM-11/12).
- Reliable service with low backup, odor, and overflow exposure (DIM-05/06).
- Whether capital improves everyday sanitation, not only regional metrics.

## Used during

- Equity, affordability, service-coverage, and tier/SLA review for municipal, small community, and local/decentralized systems (T2/T3/T4).
